== howto | marigold-yoga/anjali | Low | Done | rate=5
# Set up monthly membership invoices
C: I have 40 students on a monthly membership. Do I really have to make 40 invoices every month?
A sofia: Hi Anjali,

No. Use recurring invoices: open a student's invoice and click Make recurring, choose Monthly and a start date. Fernbill then creates and sends it each month. If students agree to it, turn on Auto-charge so their card is charged on the same day and they get a receipt.

To set them all up at once, create one recurring invoice, then use Duplicate for each student and change the client.

Sofia
