A tomas: Hi Omar,

ACH payments take 3 to 5 business days to clear. Fernbill marks the invoice Paid once the bank confirms it, and then pays out on the usual 2-day schedule. For a Tuesday payment, you'd expect it to be marked paid by Monday and in your account by Wednesday.
