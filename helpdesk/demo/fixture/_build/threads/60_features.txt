== feature | vireo-labs/noah | Medium | Done
# Custom fields in the API response
C: We added custom fields to invoices ("Contract ID", "Region"). They don't show up in the API response. Any plans to include them?
A sofia: Hi Noah,

Good news: they're already available, just not included by default. Add ?expand=custom_fields to GET /v3/invoices or GET /v3/invoices/{id} and they'll be in a custom_fields object, keyed by the field's API name.

I've also suggested that the docs mention this on the invoice endpoint page, since it's easy to miss.

Sofia
C: Oh nice, works. Thanks.

== feature | pinecrest-property-management/greg | Medium | Replied | to=sofia
# Late fee by days overdue, not a flat rate
C: We'd like late fees that increase the longer rent is overdue: $50 after 5 days, then $10 per day up to a cap. Our leases are written that way. Is that possible or planned?
A sofia: Hi Greg,

Fernbill's late fees today are either a flat amount or a percentage, applied once or repeating at a fixed interval. A tiered fee with a daily amount and a cap isn't possible yet.

The closest setup is a $50 flat fee after 5 days, then a $10 fee repeating every day. What's missing is the cap, so you'd have to watch for invoices that reach it. I've logged a request for capped late fees with your lease terms as the example.

Sofia
