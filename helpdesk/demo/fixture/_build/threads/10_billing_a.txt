== billing | bluefin-analytics/maya | Medium | Done | rate=5
# Adding 4 seats mid-cycle, how is it billed?
C: We're hiring four people next week. If I add them now, are we charged the full month for each or prorated?
A ananya: Hi Maya,

Prorated. When you add a seat, you're charged only for the days left in your current billing period, and from the next period it's part of your regular bill.

For example, if you add four seats with 12 days left in a 30-day period, you'd pay about $38 now ($24 x 4 x 12/30), then $96 more per month after that.

Ananya
