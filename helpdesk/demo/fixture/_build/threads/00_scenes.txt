# API rate limit errors during the nightly invoice sync
C: Hi,

Our nightly job that pushes patient invoices from our practice management system into Fernbill has started failing partway through. We get HTTP 429 responses after roughly 600 requests. It used to run without errors. We send about 2,400 invoices a night across our 31 locations.

Has the rate limit changed? We are on the Enterprise plan and I thought we had a higher limit.

Marcus Bell
IT Manager, Brightwater Dental Group
A yuki: Hi Marcus,

Thanks for the request IDs. You're right that the limit changed: with v3, the API moved from a per-day quota to a per-minute limit of 600 requests per account, and Enterprise accounts can have it raised. Your sync sends everything in one burst, which is why it stops at about the same point each night.

Two options while we sort out the higher limit:
- Use the batch endpoint, POST /v3/invoices/batch, which takes up to 100 invoices per request. That brings your 2,400 invoices down to 24 calls.
- Respect the Retry-After header on a 429 and resume after the wait.

I've passed this to Jamie, who handles limit increases for Enterprise accounts.

Yuki
@ jamie +20m
A jamie +3h: Hi Marcus,

Jamie here, I lead the support team. I've requested a raise to 3,000 requests per minute for Brightwater, and engineering usually applies these within one business day. I'll confirm here once it's live.

That said, the batch endpoint Yuki mentioned is the better long-term fix, since it also makes your sync finish much faster. If your developer wants a quick call to walk through it, I'm happy to set one up.

Jamie
