== Download invoices | Personal | jamie
Hi,

You can download past invoices in bulk from the Invoices list:

1. Go to Invoices and set the date filter to the period you need, for example last year.
2. Tick the checkbox at the top of the list to select every invoice in that period.
3. Click Actions > Download PDFs.

Fernbill puts the PDFs in a single zip file. For a large number of invoices it's prepared in the background, and we email you a download link when it's ready. If you'd rather have a spreadsheet, choose Actions > Export CSV instead.

Let me know if anything doesn't look right.

== Failed subscription payment | Team | Billing & Payments
Hi,

Your last Fernbill payment didn't go through. Your account stays fully active for 7 days while you sort it out.

To update your card, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, make sure pop-ups are allowed for Fernbill so the confirmation window can open. We'll retry the payment as soon as the new card is saved.

== Payout timing | Team | Billing & Payments
Hi,

Card payments are paid out 2 business days after the payment. ACH payments take 3 to 5 business days to clear first, and then follow the same 2-day schedule. You can see each payout and the payments in it under Payments > Payouts.

If a payout shows as sent but hasn't reached your bank, your bank may be holding it for review. Give them the payout reference from the Payouts page and they can usually release it the same day.
